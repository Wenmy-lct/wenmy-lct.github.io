关于我About

我是上海财经大学计算机与人工智能学院的博士研究生，研究兴趣包括具身智能、控制理论与微分几何，尤其关注迭代学习控制。

我在苏州大学完成本科与硕士阶段的学习，过往研究涉及脑机接口、信号处理、复杂网络及凸优化。我的研究经历涵盖控制算法设计、机器人路径跟随与实验平台开发。

I am a PhD student at the School of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Shanghai University of Finance and Economics. My research interests include embodied intelligence, control theory, and differential geometry, with a particular interest in iterative learning control.

I completed my undergraduate and master’s studies at Soochow University, where my work spanned brain–computer interfaces, signal processing, complex networks, and convex optimization. My research experience includes control algorithm design, robotic path following, and experimental platform development.
